Hi there! I am a final-year undergraduate at Peking University, majoring in Intelligence Science and Technology at the School of Intelligence Science and Technology, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Hao Dong and work closely with my senior collaborator Yuxing Long.

My research centers on embodied intelligence and multimodal comprehension, with a focus on vision-language navigation and spatial intelligence.

I am currently interested in multimodal large language models for developer productivity, particularly LLM-based coding agents.
